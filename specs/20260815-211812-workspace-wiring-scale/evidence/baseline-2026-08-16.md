# Baseline sweeps, 2026-08-16

Produced by `task test:scale` (`test/integration/scale/sweep_test.go`) against a
real kcp v0.32.3 with a multicluster manager engaged on every workspace.

**Load mode: synthetic.** Objects were generated for the measurement, so these
figures carry the caveat FR-039 attaches to that mode — see "Known limits".

## Reading these

Both profiles are linear with no curvature: goroutine deltas are 104/8, 208/16
and 416/32 — the same 13.0 three times over. Telemetry stayed at 20 labelled
series across 64 workspaces, so the cardinality bound holds under load.

## Known limits

These figures do **not** support a capacity statement yet, for three reasons
recorded in full at research R14:

1. **Event delivery cost is not among them.** `Load` measures issuing writes,
   which return before dispatch. The O(W) listener fan-out is invisible here.
   **FR-001 cannot be determined from this run.**
2. **One watch, not the wired ~19.** 13.0 goroutines per workspace is a floor
   for the wiring, not a figure for `core-manager`.
3. **64 workspaces, not the 256 the fixture reaches.** Anything quoted above 64
   is an extrapolation and must be labelled one.
